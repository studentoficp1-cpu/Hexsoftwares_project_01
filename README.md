# 📊 Regional Sales & Profit Data Analysis

This project involves cleaning, analyzing, and visualizing a business dataset containing regional sales and profit data. The primary objective is to derive actionable business insights and assess overall performance across different states and categories. This repository marks the completion of my first internship project!

---

## 🛠️ Key Steps Performed:

* **Data Cleaning:** Handled missing values across key columns and successfully removed duplicate records to ensure data integrity.
* **Descriptive Statistics:** Calculated fundamental metrics including Mean, Median, Mode, Skewness, and Kurtosis to understand data distribution.
* **Correlation Analysis:** Analyzed relationships between Sales, Quantity, Discount, and Profit (revealing a negative correlation of `-0.247` between Discount and Profit).
* **Pivot Tables & Charts:** Developed interactive summaries and custom dashboards (including combo charts and sorted horizontal bar charts) for clear visual impact.

---

## 📈 Key Insights & Business Takeaways:

1. **Top Performing State:** **California** emerged as the leading state, generating the highest total volume of sales.
2. **Loss-Making Product Segment:** Within the Furniture category, **Tables** show remarkably high sales volumes but are causing a substantial net **Loss** for the business.
3. **Impact of Discounts:** The correlation metrics confirm that higher discount rates are directly suppressing profit margins rather than productively driving net returns.
